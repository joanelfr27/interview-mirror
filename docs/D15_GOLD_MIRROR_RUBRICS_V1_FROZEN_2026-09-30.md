Status: **FROZEN / HUMAN-REVIEWED**

Authority boundary: these rules define desired product behaviour; they are not derived from current D15 output.

## Universal truth rules

- Evidence grounding, truth boundaries, and evidence traceability are mandatory.
- No generated interpretation becomes evidence.
- Preserve ownership semantics. Supporting, participating, implementing, managing, and leading are not interchangeable.
- Do not upgrade ownership, outcomes, scale, timing, seniority, or scope beyond cited evidence.
- D15-A maturity remains fail-closed: with canonical role context unavailable, supported threads are capped at EMERGING_PATTERN.
- Not Said Yet is thread-level and deterministic for OUTCOME, SCALE, and TIMING.
- Wording is not scored by exact string match; semantic meaning and Must/Must Not boundaries are authoritative.
