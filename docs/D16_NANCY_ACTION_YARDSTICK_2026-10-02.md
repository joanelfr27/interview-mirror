## PREP — intended English action

Choose one statutory-reporting or acquisition-accounting example from your documented work. Prepare the accounting issue, the task you personally performed, and how your contribution differed from the integration team's responsibilities. Your CV names supporting integration and implementing accounting systems; separate those roles. Identify the standard, jurisdiction and period only if you can substantiate them. Bring an anonymized source or a precise account of applying the relevant rule; otherwise keep international-standard experience unconfirmed. Do not turn system implementation into proof of IFRS expertise.

## PRACTICE — intended English action

Practise answering: “Which international accounting standard did you apply in your reporting or integration work, and what did you personally do?” Start from the chosen CV example. Explain your contribution without upgrading “supporting” to “leading”. If the standard cannot yet be established, distinguish your documented accounting work from that unverified requirement. Follow-up: “What judgment did you make, and what evidence could substantiate it?” Do not invent a rule, outcome or ownership level to complete the answer.
