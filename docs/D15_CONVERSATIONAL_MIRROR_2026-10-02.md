Show grounded and significant threads immediately when the canonical evidence supports them. When a substantive relationship is not documented, ask at most two open questions about the missing link, then rebuild the Mirror from the candidate's answer. Empty CV-derived threads are an honest state, not automatically an error or a requirement for every candidate. This changes the initial experience from guaranteed insights to evidence-based discovery; its engagement/WOW remains to be validated with users.

Questions anchor in the candidate's original words and ask what connection, if any, existed. They do not present a guessed conclusion for confirmation. No relationship, uncertainty, and lack of experience are legitimate answers. A bare yes does not establish a relational fact.

Answer path: answer text alone → existing E1 extractor → existing canonicalizer → source/atom validators → shared D15 eligibility → D15 proposer → same G and S judges → accepted thread. Keep CANDIDATE_ELICITED and ELICITED provenance; an accepted thread is grounded in candidate self-report, not independently verified. Questions/hypotheses are never included in extraction input. Retain rejected answers/atoms and diagnostics without granting support; one invalid atom must not erase other valid atoms.
