# D15 v1.1 development erratum — 2026-10-02

Status: DEVELOPMENT RULE ADDENDUM; not a new human-qualified codebook.
Base remains frozen at blob 56f753c50e50aeb8532d7e7a70eeea04f0e69255.
Prior run expectations and results remain immutable in their original commits.

1. Fix meaning before G and S. Record component claims and the explicit relationship between them. Plain conjunction of activities is an enumeration: it does not imply causation, information flow, a joint purpose, or interaction. A purpose inside one listed activity does not connect it to the other listed activity.
2. The stronger-reading rule protects grounding; it does not grant significance. Generic intersection/bridge wording may assert that two domains are connected, requiring G to license that connection. S4 still assigns S=NO when the claim provides only domain packaging, an intrinsic duty purpose or the anti-shortcut financial-data/decision-making label. A stronger generic connection is still generic. Do not weaken the connection for G, or add a substantive mechanism for S. A substantive claim specifies what passes between activities or how one informs, changes or structures the other.
3. G can be YES on an exactly evidenced enumeration while S is NO. Never require a cross-activity relationship that the proposition does not assert. Do not turn a stated purpose into an achieved outcome.
4. Only G=NO/S=YES substantive relationships without truth vetoes may become relationship questions. Ask whether the explicit link occurred, permitting no. Do not ask the candidate to reconfirm a list of activities.
5. Record raw judge verdicts separately from structural vetoes. Enumeration and packaging vetoes may reject acceptance/question routing; they never manufacture G=YES or hide an incorrect raw S=YES.

Development comparison: same fixed semantic reading, rules and controls for gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18 and gpt-4.1-2025-04-14. Each axis/model is called once. This is not qualification, and historical runs are not controlled model comparisons because prompts changed. The stronger model is not promoted to production by this experiment.
