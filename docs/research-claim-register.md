# Research Claim Register — D1–D10 and E1

Use these tags in the thesis/research record:

- **HYPOTHESIS:** product or research proposition originating from the project; not established by literature or experiment.
- **LITERATURE-SUPPORTED:** supported by an identified external source; retain the citation and population/time period.
- **EXPERIMENTALLY-VALIDATED:** tested under a predefined protocol with reported results and limitations.
- **DESIGN-ASSERTION:** architectural choice or invariant; validated by code review/structure, not empirical effectiveness.
- **UNVERIFIED:** implemented or proposed but not yet runtime-tested or empirically assessed.

## Historical continuity rule

Earlier discussions remain part of the project chronology, but they must not be presented as retrospective proof that a later hypothesis was already established. Preserve original dates and wording where possible; distinguish contemporaneous hypotheses from later literature support and later experimental validation.
