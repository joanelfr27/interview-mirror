# D15 Gold Mirror Rubrics — v1 DRAFT

**Status:** draft for human review (Thibaut). Not frozen.
**Derived from:** the hand-written Gold Mirrors and the source CV text only. Not derived from any D15 output.
**Scope of tomorrow's gate:** fields 1–8 and 10. Field 9 (story boundary) is defined now but is not scored until D15-C.

**Conventions**
- Evidence is cited by **exact source quote**, not atom ID, because atom IDs vary between runs. Traceability is satisfied when a displayed item resolves to the atoms whose source spans contain these quotes.
- **Evidence = responsibility lines only.** Summary, profile, skills lists, education and role-overview paragraphs never count towards a thread, its maturity, or its headline.
- **Maturity:** every CV in Gold Set v1 has one undated role, so no thread may exceed **Emerging**.
- Wording is never scored. A headline passes if it carries the core meaning and breaks no Must Not.

## 1. Nancy Fine

**Metadata:** CV: Nancy Fine · Gold threads: 2 (A, B) · Language: EN · Gold version: v1

### Thread A — finance continuity through business change

1. **Required evidence (Must)**
   - "Supporting acquisition accounting and financial integration activities." — involvement in acquisition-related finance work
   - "Supporting systems integration following business changes." — involvement in integrating systems after change
   - "Implementing and improving accounting systems and processes." — implementation and improvement of finance systems
2. **Core meaning (Must)** — Her work repeatedly sits where finance has to keep functioning while systems, processes or the business itself are changing.
3. **Acceptable grouping**
   - Must: the three required lines together.
   - Should not: "Managing accounting systems and financial procedures." (steady-state work; tolerated only if the headline still expresses change, and scored as reduced purity).
   - Must not: "Maintaining effective financial controls and reporting processes.", any Thread B line.
4. **Prohibited claims (Must Not)** — that she *led* acquisitions or integrations; any outcome (successful, faster, improved results); scale (size of acquisition, team, systems); number of acquisitions; dates or duration; "transformation leader", "strategic", or any seniority claim.
5. **Expected question-back (Must)** — The ownership tension: she wrote "supporting" for acquisition and integration work, but "implementing" for accounting systems. D15 must ask which she led, not decide.
6. **Expected "Not said yet" (Must)** — outcome (what changed), scale, timing.
7. **Maturity (Must)** — Emerging. Justification: 3 lines, all from one role, no dates.
8. **Expected restraint (Must Not)** — must not describe this as a career-long pattern or progression.
9. **Story boundary** — May say she works on finance through acquisitions and system changes. May not say she led them, delivered results, or did so repeatedly across employers.
10. **Traceability (Must)** — Headline, question and gaps each resolve to the three required quotes. The question must cite at least one "Supporting…" line and the "Implementing…" line.

### Thread B — financial information into management decisions

1. **Required evidence (Must)**
   - "Providing financial information to management to support business decisions." — information supplied for decisions
   - "Communicating financial information to internal stakeholders." — information communicated to stakeholders
2. **Core meaning (Must)** — Her role connects financial information to the people who make decisions.
3. **Acceptable grouping**
   - Must: the two required lines together.
   - Should not: "Preparing and analysing actual, forecast and budget financial information." (preparation, not decision-use; tolerated as reduced purity only).
   - Must not: "Managing statutory financial reporting and taxation requirements.", "Maintaining effective financial controls and reporting processes."
4. **Prohibited claims (Must Not)** — that her information changed decisions or outcomes; "strategic adviser", "business partner" or any seniority claim; the audience's level beyond "management" and "internal stakeholders".
5. **Expected question-back (Should)** — An outcome question, e.g. whether any decision changed because of the information she provided.
6. **Expected "Not said yet" (Must)** — outcome, scale (audience, frequency), timing.
7. **Maturity (Must)** — Emerging. 2 lines, one role.
8. **Expected restraint (Must Not)** — must not merge Thread B into Thread A through the shared word "financial".
9. **Story boundary** — May say she turns financial information into material management uses. May not claim influence on specific decisions.
10. **Traceability (Must)** — Resolves to the two required quotes.

### Nancy — CV-level Must Not
- No thread built on "Managing statutory financial reporting…" + "Maintaining effective financial controls and reporting…" (shared word "reporting" only).
- No evidence from the professional summary, core skills list, role-overview paragraph or education.

6. **Expected "Not said yet" (Must)** — outcome (did delays fall), timing.
7. **Maturity (Must)** — Émergente / Emerging. 3 lines, one role.
8. **Expected restraint (Must Not)** — must not treat « direction » as a connecting theme.
9. **Story boundary** — May say she finds what blocks the order flow and changes procedures across agencies. May not claim measurable improvement.
10. **Traceability (Must)** — Resolves to the required quotes; the question cites the « Déployait » and « Participait à » lines.
